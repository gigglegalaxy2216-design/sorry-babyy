Just For You 💌

Nijanga cheppalante, ninnu ila dooram chesukovadam na valla avvatledhu. Nuvvu na life lo entha important oo nenu words lo cheppalenu. Mana madhya em jarigina, ninnu hurt chesina prathi sari nenu nijanga regret avuthunna.


Nen chesina mistakes ni ardham chesukoni, avi repeat avvakunda nannu nenu change chesukovali anukuntunna. Nuvv na meedha pettukunna trust ni malli earn chesukovali anukuntunna.


Manam kalisi unna memories, mana happy moments anni naaku chala special. Nuvvu naatho unna prathi moment ni nenu chala precious ga feel avuthunna. Inka 1 year and 5 months manam kalisi undali ani naaku chala strong ga anipisthundhi.


Ninnu force cheyyalani kaadhu, nee feelings ni respect chesthune chepthunna. Nuvv konchem time teesukoni alochinchu. Nenu nijanga mana relationship ni save chesukovali anukuntunna.


Nuvv ok ante, manam malli fresh ga start cheddham. Ee sari mana relationship ni inka strong ga, trust tho, understanding tho build cheskundham.


Nuvv naak chala important. Ninnu lose avvalani nenu assalu anukovatledhu. I still want us to be together. ❤️
